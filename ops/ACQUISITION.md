# Customer acquisition plan (started 18 Nov 2026)

Goal: genuine UK buyers find Hourwright, compare watches and choose to visit an approved retailer. Free, legitimate methods only. Results are tracked in issue #26; nothing here is a forecast.

## Search: where Hourwright can realistically compete

Evidence: live search results checked 18 Nov 2026 (no search-volume tool is available, so no volumes are quoted) and the live catalogue. Generic head terms ("best dive watch under 5000") are dominated by large publishers and US-dollar pages. Hourwright's edge is UK pounds, live UK retailer prices and stock, and specific models it actually lists.

| # | Opportunity | Why | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Christmas watch gifts (for her, then for him) | Seasonal purchase intent now; catalogue has Longines, Rado, Chanel, Chopard women's watches in stock | For her: published 18 Nov. For him: brief B8, due 27 Nov |
| 2 | Model vs model: Longines HydroConquest vs Oris Aquis Date | Specific comparison with buying intent; both in stock at Goldsmiths; competing pages are mostly US-priced | Published 18 Nov |
| 3 | Dive watches under £5,000 (UK) | Strong intent; 90+ dive watches listed | Collection page live; brief B1 |
| 4 | Chanel J12 sizes and versions | Model research before purchase; about 29 J12s in stock; no commission exclusion for Chanel at Goldsmiths | Brief B7 |
| 5 | GMT and travel watches under £5,000 | Clear category intent; Longines Zulu Time and HydroConquest GMT in stock | Brief B4 |

Product pages stay `noindex` (they carry retailer copy). The indexable commercial pages are collections, brands, Journal articles and guides, and these now link to each other.
