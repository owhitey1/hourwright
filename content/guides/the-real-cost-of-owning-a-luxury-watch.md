The price on the listing is the largest cost of a watch and not the only one. These are the others, and the questions that pin them down before you buy.

## Servicing

A mechanical watch needs servicing periodically. The movement is dismantled, cleaned, lubricated and regulated, and the seals are replaced. Each brand publishes a recommended interval and a price list for its own service centres.

What moves the cost:

- **Complications.** A chronograph costs more to service than a three-hand watch.
- **Brand.** Service pricing differs widely between makers.
- **Who does the work.** Brand service centres generally cost more than independent watchmakers, but keep the service history with the manufacturer.

Before buying, look up the brand's published service prices for the model you want.

## Insurance

Check your home contents policy. Many have a single-item limit below the price of a luxury watch and cover outside the home only if the item is specified. Insurers commonly ask for the receipt and may ask for a valuation. Specialist watch and jewellery policies exist as an alternative.

## Straps and bracelets

Leather straps are consumables and will need replacing with regular wear. Brand straps are priced accordingly. If you think you will want a bracelet, buy the bracelet version at the outset, since adding one later usually costs far more than the difference between the two listings.

## Resale

Most watches sell for less second-hand than they cost new. A small number of models hold or exceed their retail price, and those are rarely available to order online. Buy a watch because you want to wear it.

What supports resale value:

- The original box, warranty card and paperwork.
- Spare bracelet links.
- A documented service history.
- An unpolished case in good condition.

## A simple way to compare

When two watches are close on price, compare them on the brand's published service cost, the length of the warranty and whether the bracelet is included. Those three figures often separate them more clearly than the specification sheet does.
