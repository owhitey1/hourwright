Longines first served as official timekeeper of the Commonwealth Games at the 1962 Perth Games, according to Commonwealth Sport, and returned in that role for Glasgow 2026. It marked the Games with a commemorative version of its HydroConquest dive watch. The edition was announced in May 2026, ahead of the Games opening in Glasgow on 23 July. Six months on, Goldsmiths still lists both sizes.

## What is different

The changes are cosmetic and specific to the Games:

- **Colour.** The dial is a teal gradient taken from the Glasgow 2026 logo, with the HydroConquest name in violet and a pink-tipped seconds hand, the other two colours of the Games identity.
- **Caseback.** The screw-down caseback is engraved with the official Glasgow 2026 logo and "Limited Edition – One of 2026".
- **Numbers.** Production is limited to 2,026 pieces in each of the two sizes, 39mm and 42mm.

## What is the same

Underneath, it is a current-generation HydroConquest. Both sizes are rated to 300 metres, with a unidirectional ceramic bezel, screw-in crown and screw-down caseback. The automatic movement, Longines' calibre L888.5, beats at 25,200 vibrations per hour and has a silicon balance spring and a power reserve of up to 72 hours. It comes on a black rubber strap with a folding clasp. Longines set the same launch price for both sizes, and Goldsmiths' current price for each is shown below.

## Our view

Commemorative editions often change only a dial colour. This one does little more, but it starts from a capable modern dive watch and keeps the full specification, so it gives up nothing to the standard model. The case for buying it rests on whether you want the Glasgow 2026 colours and caseback; if you would rather have a plain HydroConquest, the standard models sit in the same price band in our [dive watch collection](../watches/dive-watches.html).

Limited editions do not return once sold out. With 2,026 made in each size, this is a larger run than many, so treat the limitation as a detail rather than a reason to rush. The 39mm version also features in our guide to [five Longines watches under £3,000](five-longines-watches-under-3000.html).
