This week's pick is a dive watch that does one unusual thing well. Instead of a rotating bezel on the outside of the case, the Legend Diver keeps its dive-timing ring inside, under the crystal, and turns it with a second crown. Longines first used the layout in the late 1950s, and the current 39mm model brings it back in a size that suits most wrists.

## What it is

The Legend Diver is Longines' reinterpretation of its 1959 diver. The two crowns are the giveaway: the lower one sets the time, the upper one turns the internal bezel. Longines argues the internal position protects the bezel from knocks and accidental movement, the main weakness of an exposed bezel on a working dive watch.

## Why a buyer might consider it

- **Real dive-watch credentials at this price.** Longines describes the 39mm Legend Diver as certified to the ISO 6425 divers' watch standard, which requires testing beyond a plain water-resistance figure.
- **A movement built for daily wear.** The silicon balance spring resists magnetism, a common cause of a mechanical watch running fast, and the 72-hour reserve means it will still be running after a weekend off the wrist.
- **A wearable size.** At 39mm it sits between the oversized divers of the last decade and smaller vintage pieces.
- **Something different.** Most dive watches under £3,000 look alike; the twin crowns and inner bezel set this one apart.

## What to weigh up

- **The strap.** This version comes on rubber with a simple buckle. Longines also offers the watch on a steel bracelet, which costs more; our catalogue currently lists a bracelet version with a white dial.
- **Setting the bezel.** Turning a ring through a crown is slower than gripping an external bezel. For most owners, who use the bezel to time parking or cooking, this hardly matters.
- **No date.** Check whether you want a date window before you buy.
- **Vintage styling.** The painted numerals and arrow hands are a deliberate nod to the 1959 original and will not suit everyone.
