A watch is one of the few Christmas presents that can still be worn every day in twenty years. It is also easy to get wrong: too large, too formal, or a movement that does not suit how often it will be worn. These five are chosen to cover different tastes and budgets from about £2,000 to just under £5,000, from four makers. All were in stock and under £5,000 when we published; the cards show today's retailer price.

## How they compare

| Longines Mini DolceVita | 21.5 x 29mm rectangular, steel | Quartz | 30 metres | Steel bracelet |

Water resistance figures are each maker's own. A 30-metre rating means everyday splashes and hand-washing, not swimming.

## Before you choose: three questions

**What size does she wear now?** You rarely need to know her wrist measurement. Look at a watch or bracelet she already wears and likes, and compare its width with the case sizes above. As a rough guide, 28 to 30mm reads as a classic, delicate size; 33 to 34mm is a modern everyday size that suits most wrists. Our [watch size guide](../guides/watch-size-guide.html) explains how case width, thickness and lug length change the fit.

**Quartz or automatic?** Four of these five are quartz. A quartz watch keeps running in a drawer, is generally more accurate than a mechanical watch, and only needs a battery change every few years. That suits a watch worn for occasions. An automatic, like the Conquest, winds itself as it is worn and stops if left off for a few days, but it needs no battery and many people value the mechanism. Our guide to [automatic, quartz and hand-wound movements](../guides/automatic-quartz-or-hand-wound.html) covers the trade-offs.

**Jewellery or everyday?** A slim watch on a fine bracelet or leather strap is a piece of jewellery first. A sportier watch on a steel bracelet will be worn daily and shrug off knocks. Think about which she reaches for: her rings and necklaces, or her trainers.

## For a watch she will wear every day: Longines Conquest 34mm

The Conquest is Longines' sports line, and this 34mm version has a sunray blue dial, a steel bracelet with a triple-safety folding clasp, a screw-in crown and 100-metre water resistance, so it can go swimming. Longines states an automatic movement with up to 72 hours of power reserve and a silicon balance spring. Longines gives mechanical watches bought from an official dealer a five-year warranty.

Consider: it is the sportiest watch here, and at 34mm it is also the largest. As an automatic it will stop if left unworn for a few days.

## For a classic dress watch: Longines Mini DolceVita

DolceVita is Longines' rectangular collection, and the Mini DolceVita measures 21.5 by 29mm. This one has a white mother-of-pearl dial that Longines describes as snow-set, a sapphire crystal with anti-reflective coating on both sides, and a steel bracelet with a triple-safety folding clasp and push-piece opening. It is a quartz watch, so it is ready to wear whenever she picks it up.

Consider: it is rated to 3 bar (30 metres), so keep it away from the pool. Longines' warranty for quartz models is two years. A rectangular case also sits differently from a round one; if she has never worn one, compare it against a watch she owns.

## For something distinctive at a similar price: Rado Centrix Diamonds 30.5mm

Rado is known for high-tech ceramic, and this Centrix combines it with steel finished in gold-coloured PVD on the bracelet. Rado lists a 30.5mm case only 8.9mm thick, a black faceted dial with twelve diamonds and a date, a rounded sapphire crystal that runs to the edge of the case, and 50-metre water resistance. Rado offers a five-year international warranty.

Consider: the mix of gold-coloured and black is a strong look rather than a neutral one. The PVD finish is a coating over steel, not solid gold.

## For a design icon: Chanel J12 28mm

The J12 is Chanel's signature watch, made in ceramic. This 28mm version is one of the most compact J12s: a one-piece black ceramic case 8.8mm thick, a fixed steel bezel with a black ceramic ring, a black lacquered dial with rhodium-plated markers that Chanel says are set by hand, and a quartz movement. Chanel lists 30-metre water resistance and fits this reference with a textured black rubber strap and a steel pin buckle. Chanel's UK site describes a five-year warranty.

Consider: this is the rubber-strap version, not the all-ceramic bracelet many people picture when they think of a J12. A rubber strap is comfortable and casual, but check that it matches what she has in mind.

## For a playful luxury piece: Chopard Happy Sport 30mm

Happy Sport watches are known for diamonds that move freely across the dial. Chopard lists three moving diamonds on a deep black lacquered dial, a 30mm case in 18ct rose gold and steel, an onyx set in the crown, a sapphire crystal and a quartz movement. It comes on a black calfskin strap with a steel pin buckle.

Consider: at 30 metres it is not a swimming watch, and a leather strap needs care around water. It is the most expensive watch in this guide. Chopard's customer service pages give a two-year warranty from purchase at an authorised dealer.

## Buying it as a gift

Buy from an authorised retailer so the manufacturer's warranty applies, and keep the warranty card or digital certificate with the receipt. Check the retailer's own delivery cut-off dates for Christmas and its returns window before you order, especially if you are guessing the style or size; policies are the retailer's and can change. Our guide to [buying a luxury watch as a gift](../guides/buying-a-luxury-watch-as-a-gift.html) covers sizing adjustments, engraving and presentation.
